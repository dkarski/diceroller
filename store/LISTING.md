- Name: **DiceRoller**
- Languages: 63 (follows the phone's language; can be changed in Settings)

## Description (also the changelog of the first public version)
> Roll D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20, a coin or a custom die from 2 to 9999. The result appears in place of the die you
> tapped. Combine rolls to add up a set, reroll it with one tap or start a new one. Optional vibration, keep screen on
> and dark theme. 63 languages. Fully offline, with no ads or tracking.
