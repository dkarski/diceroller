# DiceRoller - Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

DiceRoller is a dice app for Mudita Kompakt, made by Daniel Karski (contact: daniel.karski5q@gmail.com).

## Summary
DiceRoller does not collect, send or share any personal data.

## What the app stores
Your settings (language, vibration, keep screen on, dark theme and coin as numbers) are stored only on your phone.
Rolls and combined sets are not saved. Settings are deleted when you uninstall the app. They are not included in
phone backups.

## Network and permissions
The app works offline. It has no internet permission, asks for no permissions and never connects to anything.
It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Links that leave the app
- Contact: tapping the address copies it; a separate link opens your e-mail app if one is installed.
  Nothing is sent by the app.
- Tip jar: shows a QR code and the Ko-fi address to open on another device. The app itself opens nothing. Tips are
  handled entirely by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy and are voluntary; they unlock nothing in the app.

## Children
The app does not collect personal data from children or any other users.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published in the app and on the app's InkApps page
with a new effective date.
